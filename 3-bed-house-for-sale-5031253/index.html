Description

This spacious three-bedroom townhouse extends to approximately 115 sq.m. and is situated in the popular Athlumney Wood development. Its generous living accommodation offers a practical layout for family life.

The property has parking to the front and enjoys an open outlook across a large green area. To the rear is a paved, low-maintenance garden with gated access.

Athlumney Wood is conveniently located for the shops, schools and amenities of Johnstown and Navan, with access to the M3 at Junction 8 nearby.

The accommodation comprises an entrance hall, living room, kitchen/dining room, guest WC, three bedrooms, one ensuite, and a family bathroom.
